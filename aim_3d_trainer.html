3D Aim Trainer

WASD to move. Space to jump. Mouse to look. Hold click to shoot. Right-click or Q to aim.

The NPC shoots back with perfect accuracy when it can see you.

Use weapons 1-5, R reload, T reset, [ / ] NPC level, P physics, G RPG arc/straight, V enemy vision, O settings.

Arena physics stays accurate while moving. Valorant/Counter-Strike physics punish moving and airborne shots. Supply drops heal and refill ammo.
